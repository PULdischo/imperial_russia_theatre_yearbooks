      changes are flagged automatically (gold outline) by comparing each entry with the previous one in the same city: number of acts or scenes, a prologue or apotheosis, creator names newly printed or no longer printed, a staging or dance credit, «соч.» becoming «либретто», and music «и другихъ». Story sources («сюжетъ …») and rewording are ignored. The flags point you to the text; they are not a ruling. * the database records the entry as new (in the entries checked so far, the Yearbook marks this with an asterisk before the entry's number). List text is the database's transcription; it has not been checked against the scans, except the entries quoted on the presentation slides for «Спящая красавица», «Донъ-Кихотъ» and «Волшебное зеркало».

      Two different ballets can share a title («Времена года», «Карнавалъ»); each is labelled with its printed genre and first season. Performances are full performances of the ballet in the Repertoire tables; excerpts (single acts, scenes or numbers) are counted separately and shown with the Excerpts switch.
