      What the productions lists print
      SeasonSt PetersburgMoscow

      Season review opens what that city's review says about the ballet that season. changes are flagged automatically (gold outline) by comparing each entry with the previous one in the same city: number of acts or scenes, a prologue or apotheosis, creator names newly printed or no longer printed, a staging or dance credit, «соч.» becoming «либретто», and music «и другихъ». Story sources («сюжетъ …») and rewording are ignored. The flags point you to the text; they are not a ruling. * the database records the entry as new (in the entries checked so far, the Yearbook marks this with an asterisk before the entry's number). List text is the database's transcription; it has not been checked against the scans, except the entries quoted on the presentation slides for «Спящая красавица», «Донъ-Кихотъ» and «Волшебное зеркало».
