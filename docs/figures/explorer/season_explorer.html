Every season at a glance

One bar per season, split into a segment per ballet, sized by its events (full and excerpt), largest first. A few wide segments means a concentrated season; many thin ones, a varied one. Gold segments were given in both cities. The figure at the right is the top 3 ballets' share. Click a season to switch to it.

Counts are events (one theatre, date and session) from the Repertoire that gave the ballet. An event that gave the ballet in full counts as full even if it also gave an excerpt; excerpt events are those with only an act, scene or number, and the excerpt's printed title is listed under the ballet. Creators come only from that season's productions-list entry for the same city, shown by role group with the person's name as identified in the research database; hover a name for the credit as printed. Where a ballet has no list entry (no list for 1905/06 or 1906/07, or the ballet is missing from that season's list) the creators column is left blank. A list entry filed under an excerpt («Оживленный садъ») is compared with that excerpt's own events. Printed totals that differ from the Repertoire are not scan-checked here. Two different ballets can share a title («Времена года»); each is labelled with its printed genre and first season.
