      Performed ballets with no music credit in any list

      Composers come from the «музыка» credits of the productions lists, linked to people in the research database. A performance counts under the composers printed in that ballet's list entry for the same season and city; where there is none, under every composer the lists credit for the ballet. A ballet with several composers (for example «Корсаръ»: Adam, Pugni, Delibes) counts in full under each, so composer totals overlap and do not add up to the Repertoire total. Excerpts (single acts, scenes or numbers) count under the composers of the ballet they come from. Two different ballets can share a title: «Времена года» is Petipa and Glazunov's ballet and, once (8 April 1909), Fokine's «хореографическая фантазія» to Tchaikovsky; each is labelled with its printed genre and first season. Data: production research database, exported 10 Nov 2026.
