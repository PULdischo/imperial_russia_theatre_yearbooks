# Distribution of the «Ежегодникъ» before Дризенъ's reform (issues 1890-91 → 1909)

Where were the volumes sent and sold before Baron Н. В. Дризенъ took the editorship
(beginning of April 1909) and relaunched the Yearbook as a periodical (first issue May 1909)?
This file collects what the RGIA Ф. 497 оп. 18 дела received so far say about it,
issue by issue and channel by channel.

**Status: finding aid, 2026-11-02; д. 808 and 1167 added 2026-11-24.** Every reading below was made by Claude (the main session
or its reading agents) on zoomed crops of the ORIGINAL photos, unless marked otherwise. RG has
not read these pages yet; treat every figure and name as a pointer to the page, not as a
reading to cite.

- **checked by Claude** = read on the scan; the line-by-line readings are in
  [`distribution_before_1909_reads/`](distribution_before_1909_reads/) (one file per batch of pages,
  with "Corrections to summary" under each page).
- **from summary, unchecked** = taken from the page triage (`docs/rgia/dela/<delo>.jsonl`) only.
- «?» after a word or figure = doubtful reading; [нрзб] = unread. Spelling is as written,
  including different spellings of one name on different pages.
- Citations: д. / л. (folio as written; "—" if none visible) / PDF page. Consecutive PDF pages
  are often two photos of the same leaf.
- Arithmetic marked "my sum" is Claude's, not the document's. Where a written total does not
  equal the sum of its lines, both are given.
- Issue/season pairs are given only where a page states them: I = 1890-91, II = 1891-92,
  III = 1892-93, IV = 1893-94 (д. 161 л. 36, PDF p. 85; д. 192 cover; д. 181 л. 13);

| Pencil check total | 2843 | 3877 | 3858 | 3793 (adds up only on part III) | 38?1? (no part sums to it; Е. and II sum to 3889) |

Two things stand out:
- Subscribers fall issue by issue: 748, 738, 729, 681, 637.
- From issue IV, more than half of each printing went straight to the store at the
  Alexandrinsky: 2362, 2157, 2300 of 3900.

The 900/640/1400 crates of II-III are the same kind of storage. On 1 March 1896, 11 sealed
boxes holding 560 copies of I, 930 of II and 640 of III (2130 in all) were handed to the
Складъ монтировочныхъ матеріаловъ (д. 181 л. 15-18, PDF pp. 30-34, checked by Claude).

The two sources do not use the same date or the same basis (1a includes Moscow; 1b is SPb only
and later), so their figures differ: for II, SPb subscribers are 748 in 1b, against 336 + 432 = 768
SPb in 1a. Neither is "the" figure.

## 2. Issue by issue

### Issue I (1890-91)

  (д. 159 л. 42, PDF p. 76). Of the 1469, «въ СПб — 1312 и Москвѣ — 157»; the Moscow figure is
  glossed «Находилось въ Москвѣ при покупкѣ „Ежегодника“» (д. 181 л. 11, PDF pp. 21-22).
  All checked by Claude.
- **The Directorate's own account of the 1469**, in three versions (д. 159; checked by Claude):
  - A, л. 13, PDF pp. 30-31 (draft): sold to various persons 37; sold to subscribers to the
    following issues at a reduced price 405 (Ц. Б. 264 + other outlets 141); free 56; destroyed
    for work on issue II 2; sent to the Moscow Контора for sale 186; on hand 783 (Ц. Б. 711,
    printing-house office 17, Photography 14, bookshops 41).
  - B, л. 11, PDF pp. 32-33 (revised): free 63 = Administration 19, contributors («сотрудникамъ»)
    32, «сборщикамъ объявленій и на разборъ для работъ по 2-му вып.» 12; on hand 778
    (Ц. Б. 706 / 17 / 14 / 41).
  - C, л. 20, PDF pp. 40-41 (fair copy, figures overwritten to agree with B).
  - All three versions note that about 130 copies («около ста тридцати») had been sold in
    Moscow, the printing house, the Photography and the bookshops, the exact number to follow
    when those outlets reported.
- **Later SPb ledger of issue I** (д. 181 л. 11, PDF pp. 21-23, checked by Claude; to 1 Jan 1898):
  - Sold at various prices on the day subscription opened for III 392, IV 61, V 82, VI 53.
  - Sent to Moscow 154 (144 on 7 Dec 1892; 10 on 20 Dec 1896).
  - Free 11; to store 212 (pencil «Ящикъ? №1 — 320»); to Е. А. Морозова 25.
  - The written total 1310 does not equal the lines (my sum 990).
- **Another stock ledger** (д. 159 л. 38, PDF pp. 68-69, checked by Claude):
  - Sold with issues II-V: 238 + 26 + 26 + 24 = 314. On hand 605.
  - Written check sum 1349 (my sum 1339); «37 лишнихъ экз.»
  - Note: «не считая Варшавы и посланныхъ наложеннымъ платежомъ…»
- **Moscow** (д. 164, PDF pp. 6-9, checked by Claude; stock to 1 Dec 1893):
  - 78 in the Moscow Контора.
  - 18 issued to subscribers of III at 2 р. 50 к.
  - 2 to the Губернская типографія at 2 р. 25 к.
  - 58 left.
- **Moscow's own book** (д. 808 л. 1-3, PDF pp. 2-6, checked by Claude; reads file 11):
  - At the purchase, 424 copies were in Moscow. 123 of these had been sold (114) or given free (9)
    «въ пользу издателей», leaving 301 «казенныхъ» copies; SPb had 1168.
  - Of the 301: 223 sold by 1 Dec 1893 for 489 р. 60 к. «по разнымъ цѣнамъ», 78 left (отчетъ I).
    By 1 June 1895 a further 40 were sold for 103 р. 50 к. and 1 was given free, leaving 37
    (отчетъ II).
  - The 1469 reconciled (д. 808 PDF p. 4): Типографія to Ц. Б. 1260; 8 free to «Бернсенъ?»
    («при сборѣ объявленій по 2-му вып.»); 49 (struck line); 152 left in the Типографія.
    Ц. Б. 1160 + Тип. 152 = SPb 1312; Moscow 157 + 144 = 301.
  - The 301 does not match the «отправлено въ Московскую Контору» 186 of д. 181 л. 1-2 and
    д. 159 л. 13 (§6, point 7).
- **A Moscow seller's remittance** (д. 1167 л. 1-2, 8, PDF pp. 2-9, 23-25, checked by Claude;
  reads file 12):
  - On 14 Dec 1892 К. Здобновъ in Moscow sent 72 р. 90 к. «за проданные мною экземпляры» of
    issue I, by a transfer from the Контора Юнкера in envelope № 1256, in Молчановъ's name.
  - Hearing nothing, he wrote again on 16 Jan 1893. К. А. Петровъ of the Central Library replied
    on 18 Jan that the money had arrived; Молчановъ had not written «по болѣзни?». Петровъ asked
    how many copies Здобновъ had had, how many were left, and whether Левенсонъ's copies were
    counted in his.
  - Здобновъ answered on 22 Jan with an enclosed расчетъ (not in the дело). The only figures in
    the letter: he had sent 20 copies to the Губернская типографія and got 6 back from them. For
    the типографія's accounts he referred to the Moscow Контора's отношеніе of 11 Nov 1892
    № 2102 (the p. 15 docket reads «№ 2112?»).
- **Free copies of I**: see §3.1.

## 4. Changes over time

All from the checked figures above; interpretation is marked.

1. **1890-92.** The Directorate bought issue I as a finished edition (1469 copies) and sold it
   off over five years, mostly at a reduced price to people subscribing to the next issue.
2. **1892-94 (II-III).** A subscription system centred on the Central Library and the
   Typography, with staff paying by monthly salary deductions through the Касса Министерства.
   Moscow sold through its Контора and two private printing houses. Print runs rose to 2900,
   then 3900.
3. **1894-96 (IV-V).** The volume was split into three supplement books and the main volume,
   released months apart. Copies went on commission to the four box offices and the
   Photography. Receipt books were numbered by outlet. A Warsaw agent worked for one free copy.
   St Petersburg subscribers fell each year (748 → 637 over II-VI) while the run stayed at 3900,
   so more than half of each part went into store (§1b).
4. **1896-1902.** Moscow took 700 per part. The box offices and the Moscow Office kept receipt
   books (IX-X). Free copies went to new bodies (the Театральное справочно-статистическое бюро).
5. **1904-08.** Subscription by salary deduction continued, by troupe (XII). Annual income
   1906-08 per Дризенъ: 1878, 16?6, 843 р. (*Interpretation:* falling in 1908.)
6. **1909 (retrospective).** Дризенъ inherited a large unsold stock. The Смѣтная комиссія
   (д. 166 л. 2, PDF p. 7, 23 Oct 1910) says demand «судя по числу подписчиковъ и размѣру
   розничной продажи, остался, повидимому, на прежнемъ уровнѣ», and that overruns had been
   «хроническое явленіе и до его реформы». The commission gives no pre-reform subscriber
   count or credit figure.
