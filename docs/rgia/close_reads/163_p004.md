Notes:
- Debtor: «дворянина Петра Осиповича Б[о/а]ртновскаго» (gen.). The second letter of the surname, in both places on
  this form, could be о or а; the редакція's own copy (Д. 168 p. 11) writes «Бортновскій» clearly.
- The judgment is old: a justice of the peace's writ of 23 Nov 1904, № 1461, for 104 р. 51 к. with interest plus
  85 к. costs, served on the редакція in April 1909, i.e. at the very start of Дризенъ's editorship.
- The bailiff's statement that Бортновскій was paid «по службѣ помощникомъ редактора» is the creditor's claim, not
  evidence that he held the post; the редакція denied it the same day (p. 6; Д. 168 p. 11).
- Plaintiff's name and the court's участок are uncertain; the bailiff's surname («Михѣевъ?») too.
- Corrects the triage summary only by adding the full name, the 1904 date of the writ and the plaintiff.
