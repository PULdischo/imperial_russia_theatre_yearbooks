Notes:
- **«Чекетти /кв. № 126/»** is clear, with no initial, first name or troupe. The Roster has *two* SP ballet artists of
  that name every season 1890-91–1899-00: Генрихъ Цезаревичъ (also second ballet master, and from 1893-94 a school
  teacher as «Энрико») and Жозефина Валентиновна (query logged 2026-11-24). The page does not say which; RG's decision.
- «кв.» is presumably the receipt (квитанція) number. The run 121–127 (old subscribers) and 653–657 (new) suggests two
  receipt books.
- «К. А. Петровъ» (head of the Central Library) gets one copy in the free/commission columns, as on p. 110.
- Day 22 and days 18–19 have no entry on this page.
