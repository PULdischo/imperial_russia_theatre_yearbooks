## The two «отъ Шк…» entries (bookbinder-surname question; for RG)

Letter by letter, at 4× zoom, both entries show the same short word: «Ш», «к», a small loop like
an «е», a round letter, then a final stroke that ends in a hook below the line («ъ» or «р»?). It
is an abbreviation, not a full surname. On its own I cannot read it with certainty.

Evidence from the same дело, also checked by Claude on the originals (PDF p. 8 = `pages/o007`):
- p. 8 is a register of the same 2nd-issue receipts, numbered by waybill: 1473 / 11 Марта 93 / 250;
  1490 / 23 / 1250; 1495 / 27 / 50; 1516 / 10 Апр. / 133; total 1683. Right of the total it has the
  same abbreviation, «Отъ Шк[…]», in the same letter shapes.
- Directly below, a heading in the same hand writes the name out in full: «Отъ Шкварова». In that
  hand «в» is the same small «е»-like loop, and «р» has a descender.
- Over that heading is pasted a letter on printed letterhead: «ПЕРЕПЛЕТНАЯ МАСТЕРСКАЯ
  А. М. ШКВАРОВА, Офицерская ул., д. № 8» The earlier reading in `06_d181_a.md` has the letter on p. 9 signed «А. Шкваровъ»; I did not
  re-check p. 9 here.
- So the abbreviation on p. 28 matches, stroke for stroke, the one p. 8 uses for the same
  deliveries (250 on 11 March, 1250 on 23/24 March, 50 on 27 March). Directly beneath it, p. 8
  writes the name out in full.

What the two pages do *not* agree on: p. 28 dates the 1250 copies to the 24th and the last delivery
to «14 Апрѣля», 132 copies; p. 8 has the 23rd and «10 Апр.», 133. p. 28 also counts 500 + 150
from the Typography and 3 bound copies, which p. 8's binder list leaves out (p. 8 totals 1683 from
the binder alone; p. 28 totals 2335 from all sources).

Whether the binder is to be recorded as Шкваровъ is RG's decision; nothing has been changed elsewhere.

Notes:
- «Управл. Конторою?»: the earlier reading has «Конторы»; the ending is cramped.
- Name readings in the free list agree with the earlier reading of p. 29. All still carry "?"
  where marked: they are fast-hand surnames, not checked against any other source.
