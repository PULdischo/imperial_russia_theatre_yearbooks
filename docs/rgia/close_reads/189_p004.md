# Д. 189 (PDF p. 4) — close read, checked by Claude 2026-11-24

The редакція's incoming register for 1898, first page: entries 1–11. Printed form «№ входящій | Чис. |
СОДЕРЖАНІЕ»; clear clerk's hand, legibility good. «1898 г.» is written above no. 1 in the number column.

(The amounts in nos. 10 and 11 are underlined.)

Notes:
- **No. 5:** «Программа балета „Фея куколъ"» is clear. The first letter of «Фея» is a looped «Ф».
  The register gives no theatre, date or sender.
- In the Repertoire, «Фея куколъ» is given only in Moscow from 1896-97 to 1900-01, and first in SP
  in 1902-03 (query logged in `docs/query_log.md`, 2026-11-24). The three entries above no. 5 come
  from the Moscow Контора. So a Moscow programme is likely, but the register does not say so.
- **No. 6:** «балета» is struck and «оперы» written after it: the clerk first wrote «балета» again.
- **No. 7:** the bracket reads «(3 экз…-/пл.? и конвертъ)». The page summary's «3 copies +
  original» rests on reading the last word as «подлинникъ»; I read «конвертъ». The first word in
  the bracket is cramped at the edge.
- **No. 9:** «Рукопись „М. И. Петипа"» is clear. The register does not say whether it is a
  manuscript *by* or *about* Petipa.
- **No. 10:** Angerer & Göschl (Vienna), in florins and kreuzers. The kreuzers figure looks like
  «17»; the page summary had no kreuzers.
- **No. 11:** the surname looks like «Яблонскаго», but the first letter is uncertain. The kopecks
  look like «27», not the summary's «25».
