Notes:
- **No. 21:** «А. Е. Молчанова» and «Антокольскому» are clear. The vowel before «льскому» could be
  «о» or «а», but the word is otherwise secure. The register does not say what the 30 р. were
  paid for. «уплоченныя» is spelt as written.
- **No. 20 (tail):** the second subscriber's name is pinched. It begins «Т», ends «…ева» and has
  four or five letters between.
- **No. 22:** the word split across the line end reads «Гапон-» / «-фел…скаго». I can't tell
  whether «г.» means «господина» (a surname) or «города» (a town adjective, e.g. a provincial
  subscriber). Unresolved. «подписателя» is spelt as written.
- **No. 23:** the sender reads «Гросвенора?». The year is written so that it looks like «128»,
  with the 9 cramped or missing; 1898 fits the register. «Ліонскій кредитъ» is clear.
- **No. 24 (binder):** the surname sits jammed against the right edge with a letter written
  above it. I can see «Ш», «к», a crowded middle with a stroke above, and «р». The page summary's
  «Шкваровъ?» fits these strokes, but on this photo I can't read the middle letters. That is
  for RG to decide (open bookbinder-surname question; compare Д. 181 p. 28, and Шкваровъ's own
  letterhead on Д. 181 pp. 8–9 in `docs/rgia/distribution_before_1909_reads/06_d181_a.md`).
  The underlined first word of the next line reads «по?».
- **No. 25:** «115 экз.» is circled in the original.
- The «Чис.» column runs 31, 3, 3, 4, 5: no. 21 is 31 January, nos. 22–25 are 3–5 February 1898.
