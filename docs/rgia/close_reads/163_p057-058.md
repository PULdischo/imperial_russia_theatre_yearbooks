and table, p. 58 the table again and the foot. Calligraphic clerk's hand, legibility good. Printed/stamped
number «4422»; pencil «№ 12» in the top-left corner; folio «30»; round stamp «С.-Петербургская Контора Императорскихъ
Театровъ 7 ІЮЛ 1904». A large diagonal pencil word across the head, «Удержано?», with a big «Р» beside it.
