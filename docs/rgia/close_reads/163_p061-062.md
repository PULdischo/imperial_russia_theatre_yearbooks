Notes:
- **Signature «За редактора»:** the clearest of five. A capital «П.», then «Бартн» clearly, then a run of minims ending
  in a long descender and a flourish. «П. Бартн[овскій?]» fits; I can't read the ending. The same signature
  appears on pp. 60, 63, 64 and 65. The printed Roster lists «Бартновскій, Петръ Осиповичъ», a Контора official, every season
  1895-96–1911-12 (query logged 2026-11-24, see `163_p004.md`); p. 68 also deducts 2 р. from «Бартн[о/е]вскаго» «по
  Управленію». Whether the signer is that man is RG's decision.
- «Шуровъ, Г.» is bracketed and circled; Стравинская's and Серебряковъ's amounts are circled. Серебряковъ has «до Сентяб.»
- Opera troupe: not cross-checked against the Roster.
