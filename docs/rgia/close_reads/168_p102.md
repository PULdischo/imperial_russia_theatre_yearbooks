(c) What it says
The редакція answers the Хозяйственное Отдѣленіе's query about the remarks of the Ministry of the Court's Контроль (its отзывъ of 26 June, No. 2469). A theatrical season spans two calendar years, so material prepared for the issues at the start of a season is used at its end. In 1909, therefore, material was bought for the Yearbook that was used only in 1910. The sentence continues on fol. 60об («Только опредѣленную сумму …»).

(d) Corrections to 168.jsonl
- The summary is accurate. One refinement: it is material *bought* («были закуплены») in 1909 and used in 1910, not material «received».
- The page is marked «Копія».
