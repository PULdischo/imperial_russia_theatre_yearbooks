Notes:
- 4в: I read «на разрѣзъ?», not «на разборъ» as in the earlier reading (02_d159_accounts.md). At
  3× zoom the second-to-last letter has a tall stem with a crossbar, which fits «ѣ» and not «б».
  The same formula, «сборщикамъ объявленій и на разрѣзъ для работы по 3му вып.», is in the issue-2
  account (`06_d181_a.md`). On that reading, the 12 copies went to advertisement collectors and
  were cut up as working material for the 2nd issue.
- The struck phrase in item 4 seems to use the same word, «разрѣзано?». The earlier reading had
  «разобрано».
- The word struck out after «Москву и» is short and cancelled heavily. «сданныхъ» (the word in draft A) fits.
- In item 1 the struck figure before 37 also looks like 37.
- The pencil «?» in the right margin is level with item 3 (Moscow, 186).
- p. 33 adds nothing that p. 32 lacks except item 5г, the total and the Примѣчаніе.

Corrections to the page summaries: folio is «11» (pencil), not «8?»; p. 33 is the lower half of this
leaf, not a clean copy of p. 31; 4в is «Сборщикамъ объявленій и на разрѣзъ? для работъ по 2му вып.»
(advertisement collectors, plus copies cut up for work on the 2nd issue).
