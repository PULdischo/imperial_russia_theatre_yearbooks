Notes:

(a) Figures
- «2000 р.»: sure (four clear digits plus «р.»).
- «17/т. рублей» (17 thousand roubles, what the editor had asked for in his 1910 estimate): sure of «17»; checked at 2x. The «/т.» abbreviation for тысячъ is clear.
- «семи книжекъ въ 1909 г.» and «въ 1910 г. выйдетъ восемь»: sure of 7 (written as a word), 1909, 1910 and «восемь».
- «1910» above «текущій» and in «смѣтѣ 1910 г.» / «смѣта на 1910 г.» / «кредитъ 1910 г.»: sure.

(b) Doubtful readings
- «(не представляется возможнымъ?)»: «представ…» is clear; the third word is less so. The sense: an exact sum cannot be given, only an estimate.
- Lines 6-10 are the most heavily reworked. I could not read «Будучи [нрзб]-/[нрзб] по существу» (perhaps «согласнымъ»), the insertions over «однако, къ …», or most of the struck line.
- «достаточныхъ? [на?] нынѣшній?»: the first word looks like «достаточн…», the second like «нынѣшній» or «истекшій».
- «пе-/рерасходъ?»: read from «пе-» at the line end plus «р…расходъ». Moderately confident.
- «и потому?» and «не относилъ?»: the second word looks like «отнесъ/относилъ».
- The interlinear note «и въ виду … Д-ціи экстренно?» is mostly unreadable.
- «бы вступиться?» above «Хозяйственное Отдѣленіе»: uncertain.
- «ограниченіямъ?»: the word is written below the struck one at the end of the text. «по смѣтнымъ» is clear. The text breaks off with «..». There is no signature on this page; fol. 61 (p.104) begins a new letter, № 164.

(c) What it says
The exact sum spent in 1909 on material used in 1910 cannot be stated, but the editor estimates it at 2,000 roubles. The Контроль wants to charge it to the 1910 estimate, i.e. have the Directorate cover it from the 1910 allocation. The editor half-concedes the principle, but objects that the Directorate gives him no extra means. The 1910 estimate came out well below what he had proposed: he had asked for 17,000 roubles, reckoning on eight issues in 1910 against seven in 1909. He asks the Хозяйственное Отдѣленіе to press the Контроль to approve the overspend (?) and not to charge any sum against the 1910 credit, which his estimate cannot bear.

(d) Corrections to 168.jsonl
- «17 т. рублей» is confirmed: it is the sum the editor himself requested for 1910, on the basis of eight issues.
- «2000 р.» is the editor's own approximate estimate of the 1909 spending on 1910 material, not a figure set by the Контроль.
- The summary says the Directorate «gave no extra means for the following year». More exactly: the 1910 estimate as granted came out «значительно меньше» than he had proposed.
