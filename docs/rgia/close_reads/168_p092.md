Notes:

(a) Figures
- «Исх. 67» (header number): sure; the «19  г.» year is printed on the form and left blank.
- «25 р. въ мѣсяцъ» (the secretary's salary): sure.
- «5 книгъ»: sure (written large, over a struck word).
- «8 вспомогательныхъ»: fairly sure. The 8 is large and looped and may have been added in the gap.
- Interlinear line «… входящ. 150»: «150» fairly sure. The number after «исход. №» is overwritten and looks like 2 over 21 or 24. Not readable; I don't trust it. The digit after «За» (number of months) is also unreadable: 3, 7 and 8 all fit.
- Margin: the pencil «54» top centre is the folio. The «57», «55» and «№ 102» on the right belong to the edge of the next leaf showing in the photo, not this page.

(b) Doubtful readings
- «пріемъ? подписки»: the first word could be «трудъ», «счетъ» or «дѣло». The phrase is clearly «… подписки», i.e. subscription work.
- «конторскихъ?»: could also be «основныхъ»; it begins with a looped к/о.
- «журналъ? сталъ? ежемѣсячнымъ?»: the word shapes fit «ж…ъ с…ъ еже­мѣсячн…». The sense (the Yearbook now issued in monthly parts) agrees with p.103 (7 issues in 1909, 8 in 1910).
- «(увеличенной? смѣтѣ?) увеличило? работу по кредиту?»: low confidence. «по кредиту» could be «по крайней [мѣрѣ]».
- The struck line at the foot probably began a request («прошу Контору Импер. Т-въ …»), which continues on fol. 54об.

(c) What it says
The editor tells the Kontora that the редакція's office work is done by a single secretary on 25 roubles a month. The secretary registers papers, keeps the accounts, handles the correspondence and the subscriptions, and keeps 5 (office?) books and 8 auxiliary ones. That was manageable when the Yearbook came out once a year. Now it comes out in (roughly monthly?) parts under the new reform, and the work has grown. An interlinear note gives a count of incoming papers, 150, over some number of months. The request itself is on fol. 54об.

(d) Corrections to 168.jsonl
- The summary has «5 books». More exactly it is 5 books (конторскихъ?/main) plus 8 auxiliary ones, 13 in all.
- New: the interlinear workload figure «входящ. 150».
- New: the statement that the Yearbook formerly appeared once a year and is now (apparently) monthly.
- The draft is on the printed «Для памяти» form. The year is blank on the form, but the sequence puts it after Исх. 66 of 18 March 1910.
