# Д. 159, PDF pp. 90-115 — day-by-day issue registers, «4-ый выпускъ» (checked by Claude, finding aid only)

Read order: the register on pp. 100-115 (Dec 1894 – Apr 1895) is chronologically FIRST; pp. 90-97 continue/follow it (Apr 1895 – spring 1896). Pages are written up in PDF order 100→115, then 90→97.

## Column structure (pp. 100-115, same on every sheet)
Heading at left: «4-ый выпускъ» + year (p.100, 102: «1894 г.»/«1894 года»; pp.104, 106, 108, 110, 112, 114: «1895 года»/«1895 г.» — the year in the heading follows the calendar year of the entries).
Ruled columns: date | name | marker letter (к / д / р.) | group «Безплатно и на коммиссію» subdivided «1 | 2 | 3 | Ед.?» | group «Подписчикамъ» subdivided «1 | 2 | 3 | Ед.?».
- The 4th sub-column head is written «Ед.» or «Еж.» (abbreviation ambiguous on every sheet; plausibly Ежегодникъ — not read as such).
- Marker letters as written: «к» (newspapers, Moscow, Молчановъ, Афиматовъ, Смирновъ), «д» (troupe batches, Пойгинъ, по Управленію, and the single free copies to Ларошъ/Чайковскій etc. on 28 Dec), «р.» (single free copies 30 Jan 1895). Meaning not stated on the page.
- A small «√»/«v» tick follows most subscriber «1» entries (ink); red/brown pencil ticks against free copies on pp.105-106.
- «кв. №» = number after the name, written «(кв. № N)» or «/ кв. № N /».
