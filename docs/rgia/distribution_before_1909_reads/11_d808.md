# РГИА ф. 497 оп. 18 д. 808 — «Книга прихода и расхода экземпляровъ», issues I–V, Moscow (1892–95)
Scan-check by Claude, 2026-11-24 (finding aid; RG to verify). PDF pages 1–13, one photo per leaf
side. Read on the full-resolution originals, with zoomed crops of the opening line of p. 2 and
the free-copy line of p. 4. The pages marked "from summary" were not re-read.

**What the book is.** It is not the copy ledger for issues VII onwards that the report guessed.
It is a thin ruled book that opens with the Directorate's 1892 purchase of issue I. It then gives
each issue a pair of facing pages: «СПб.» on the left and «Москва» on the right. Only the Moscow
pages are filled in, from two Moscow reports (отчетъ I to 1 Dec 1893; отчетъ II to 1 June 1895).
The St Petersburg pages are blank apart from «Поступило 1168» under issue I. The book stops at
issue V, for which Moscow had sent no money by 1 June 1895. All the arithmetic below was checked
by Claude ("my sum"); every written total agrees with its lines unless a discrepancy is noted.

### Д. 808, л. 1, PDF p. 2 — the purchase of issue I and the Moscow stock
Legibility: good.
- «10 октября 1892 г. разрѣшено ст. секр. Петровымъ? пріобрѣтеніе 1469 экз. 1го в[ыпуска].»
  The surname is clear as written, «Петровымъ», and «ст. секр.» is clear. Who is meant is NOT
  established: it may be «ст[атсъ]-секр[етаремъ]» (a court rank) or some other title, and it is not
  necessarily К. А. Петровъ of the Central Library.
- «Въ Москвѣ находилось экз. 1-го вып.: 125 / 144 / 155 — всего 424 экз.» (my sum 424 ✓)
- «Изъ нихъ продано было въ пользу издателей и выдано по ихъ распоряженію безплатно:
  114 (продано) / 9 (выдано безплатно) — 123 экз.»
- «Казенныхъ экз. было въ Москвѣ — 301. / —"— въ СПб. — 1168. / 1469.» (my sums: 424 − 123 = 301 ✓;
  301 + 1168 = 1469 ✓)
- Pencil in the right margin: «301 / 144 / 157» (301 − 144 = 157).
- Last line, continued overleaf: «Изъ 301го Московскаго экз. къ 1 Декабр. 1893 г.»
Corrections to summary: none of substance. Note that "въ пользу издателей" means the 123
copies sold or given away in Moscow before the purchase were the private publishers' business,
not the Directorate's.

**Agreement with the other дела (my comparison):**
- Issue I: 301 crown copies in Moscow = 157 already there at the purchase + 144 sent
  7 Dec 1892, which agrees with д. 181 л. 11 and д. 162. 78 on hand at 1 Dec 1893 = д. 164.
  BUT д. 181 л. 1-2 and д. 159 л. 13 say «отправлено въ Московскую Контору» 186, not 301. The
  two figures may count different things, or 186 may be a slip; not resolved.
- Issue II: 564 received (555 + 9) against the SPb ledger's 565 shipped. The ledger's 565 is
  150 + 405 = 555, plus 10 sent in Dec 1896, after this report. 280 on hand at 1 Dec 1893 = д. 164.
- Issues III, IV: 400 each by June 1895, against 410 shipped (the extra 10 again went in
  Dec 1896).
- **Moscow totals to 1 June 1895, issues I–IV (my sums):** sold 263 + 375 + 328 + 265 = 1231;
  free 1 + 22 + 10 + 23 = 56; money 3242 р. 70 к.
