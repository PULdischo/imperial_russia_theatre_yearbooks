# Graduates audit (issue #136) -- 2026-10-06

Scope: the 51 Graduates pages of 1890-91..1909-10 (1910-11's 2 pages were already read in full by two readers in issue #135). 495 stored rows.
Method (as for TheaterSchoolStaff): 6 blind readers transcribed every printed block of every page (`reader_reports/`, `PROMPT.md`, `bundles/`); `parse_reports.py` + `compare.py` matched them to the stored rows; a targeted second blind read re-checked every delicate letterform (ь/ъ, Ш/Щ, д/л, и/і, ѣ) before anything was edited; `apply_graduates_fixes.py` applied the changes with old values asserted; `relink_graduates.py` renumbered person_link (no later row shifted, all removals were on pages holding only non-person rows).

Scope rulings (RG, 2026-10-06): the Graduates source is BALLET-department graduates only (drama-course graduates are out of scope by design); the near-empty Moscow pages 1903-04 p001 and 1901-02 p001 are complete as printed (the school reports omit graduate information).

## Result
- **25 non-person rows removed** (enrolment counts such as "Воспитанниковъ 26", statistics paragraphs, the 1888 statute preamble, section headings, and a phantom "Бекъ" taken from a posting-sentence exception clause) -- they were each linked to a **phantom person** (13 persons, e.g. "ученицы", "Ученицы и ученики"); all 13 are gone.
- **School repair, 168 rows:** 36 Moscow continuation-page rows (1892-93 p003, 1896-97 p003, 1898-99 p002) were stored under the St Petersburg school; 13 rows of 1895-96 p003 had no school at all; 35 rows of 1890-91 carried photo captions / whole sentences / the troupe name as `institution` and 6 Moscow drama rows carried the St Petersburg school; generic "Императорскія Театральныя Училища" headings replaced by the school. `institution` is now one of two clean labels per row.
- **Name corrections (all confirmed by two independent blind reads):** Галать->Галатъ, Шипановъ->Щипановъ, Сергій->Сергѣй (Ѳеоктистовъ), Карсаѣина->Карсавина, Кляштъ->Кякштъ, Леонтъевъ->Леонтьевъ, Іліодоръ->Иліодоръ, Лилія->Лидія, Щернваль->Шернваль, Кульгинъ->Кулыгинъ; four printed ordinals restored (Матвѣева 3-я, Эрлеръ 1-я, Легатъ 2-й, Солянниковъ 2-й); two glued first-name+patronymic split (Уракова, Кочетовская); first name stored as printed "Алексѣ" (Усачевъ 1909-10; print truncation).
- **Dates:** Тихомировъ (1892-93) corrected to Moscow, 1893-09-01 (the printed exception clause); the Жуковъ exam condition moved from rank_or_title to the note.
- **Not changed (decisions):** worn-type "д" printed as a foot-less glyph (1904-05 and 1905-06 St Petersburg: Належда / Алексанлръ x3, and the 1905-06 "Лидія") is kept as the intended д; the 1908-09 drama graduates (9 names) stay out of scope; the 28 drama-course graduates of 1890-91 (p005/p006) were REMOVED on RG's instruction (see `remove_drama_graduates.py`, issue #136 addendum).
