# Review people not in the rosters — classified, 2026-10-09

The 32 forms (54 occurrences) left after the matcher fixes. They are NOT one
question. Sorted by what they actually are, because only one group is a real
"should this person exist?" decision for RG.

Data: `docs/eval/review_people_not_in_rosters.csv`.

## A — a spelling variant of someone already present (2)

Both scan-checked. Neither is a missing person.

| printed | the database has | |
|---|---|---|
| `Соляниковъ` | `Солянниковъ` | the review prints ONE н, the rosters two. Source variation, same man. |
| `Голейзовскій` | `Галейзовскій, Кассіанъ Карловичъ` | **Kasyan Goleizovsky.** The review prints о; the rosters print а — and the *Graduates* list prints `Голейзовская` for his sister, so the source varies with itself. He enters the roster in 1909-10; this 1908-09 mention is him as a **воспитанникъ**, a year earlier. |

A third, `Балащева`, turned out to be a transcription error — the scan reads
`Балашева` — and is **fixed**; it now resolves to Александра Балашова.

The Goleizovsky case is the one worth a ruling: linking `Голейзовскій` to him
means accepting the о-spelling as a variant of the roster's а-spelling. The
evidence is on the page, not in the pipeline.

## B — pre-1890 figures in retrospectives (9 forms, 21 occurrences)

**These can never be in the rosters, which begin in 1890-91.** The largest
block is a single passage: 1895-96 SP ballet pp. 004–008 is a retrospective on
the **3 December 1864 premiere of «Конекъ-горбунокъ»**, quoting the journal
«Русская Сцена» (1864, № 11). `Троицкій`, `Ефремова`, `Зайцева` and `Лядова`
are its 1864 cast. `Лядова 2-я` is from the 1859 premiere cast of «Парижскій
рынокъ»; `Манохина` from the first cast of «Конекъ-горбунокъ».

Adding them as roster persons would be wrong. If they should exist at all it is
as a separate population — the same decision the opera composers raised.

## C — not ballet people at all (4)

`Орловская`, `Игнатьева` (хористки), `Соколовскій` (хористы) and `Баулина` sit
in **roster-change paragraphs** — "Оставили службу…" — that bleed onto a ballet
review's first page from the opera or drama section above it. Opera chorus and
drama staff. They belong to the deferred opera pass, not here.

## D — current-season dancers genuinely absent (17 forms, 27 occurrences)

The real worklist, and the only group where "should this become a person?" is
a live question. Each is named in a cast list of its own season with the
family's other gender already in the database:

`Воскресенская` 5x · `Барковская` 3x · `Титова` 3x · `Шульгина` 2x ·
`Терентьева` 2x · `Харитонова` · `Зыбина` · `Лядова` · `Галактіонова` ·
`Кусковъ` · `Шеншина` · `Лопухина` · `Ячменевъ` · `Громовъ 1-й` ·
`Павловской 2-й` · `Петровскій` · `Рождественская`

Two notes on individuals. `Петровскій` (1910-11 Moscow) plays a **судебный
слѣдователь** — a drama role, so he is probably group C with a part rather than
a dancer. `Рождественская` (1912-13) is in a graduate list from the
Diaghilev-era cohort that the rosters stop short of, like Бакстъ and Коутсъ in
the general unknown queue.

Ordinals are worth weighing: `Воронина 1-я`, `Громовъ 1-й` and `Павловской 2-й`
carry the roster's own numbering convention, which is some evidence they WERE
on a roster the corpus has not captured, rather than guests.
