ANSWER TO THE QUESTION: on all five pages Главный гардеробъ and Мѣстные гардеробы are (b) printed under Отдѣлъ гардеробный (rank T2 < T1; no T1 heading separates them from it; in 1895-96 and in SPb p001->p002 they follow Парикмахеры directly). Caveat: the print shows no indentation or bracket; within the department there are two kinds of children: occupation headings (T3 letter-spaced Костюмеръ/Костюмерша/Парикмахеръ directly under T1) and the two named sub-sections Главный гардеробъ / Мѣстные гардеробы (T2, visually ABOVE the occupation headings in size, though printed after them).
