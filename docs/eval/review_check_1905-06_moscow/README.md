# 1905-06 Moscow Drama + Opera reviews: ballet/dance and special-evening check (2026-10-09)

Source: Ежегодникъ императорскихъ театровъ, 1905-06, Part 1 (HathiTrust `mdp.39015069024134`, University of Michigan copy, Google scan, images seq 175-248 = printed folios 139-212; Moscow Drama folios 141-181, Moscow Opera 183-208). RG downloaded the page images; they are NOT stored in the repo (Google asks that images not be re-hosted). Six blind readers each quoted every ballet/dance and special-evening mention on ~13 pages (`reader_reports/report_N.txt`: one line per page, verbatim quotes); the key pages were then read by hand (folios 192, 194, 206, 207).

## Findings
1. **None of the five annotation-only special evenings of 1905-06 Moscow is in the Drama or Opera review** (20 Feb and 2 Mar, Большой, Guards/Army/Navy mutual-aid society; 25 Feb Малый, Evangelical charity for the poor; 22 Mar Малый, Rogozhskoe branch of the Ladies' Charity; 23 Mar Малый, Evangelical school), nor the 19 and 21 March invalids' concerts. The opera chronicle (folios 206-207) runs 25 Jan, 29 Jan, 30 Jan ... 12 Feb ("для закрытія оперныхъ представленій передъ Великимъ постомъ"), then 4 Apr, 5, 14, 15, 26, 27 Apr; the Drama chronicle (folios 179-181) likewise covers only the troupes' own performances (season closing 12 Feb, spring opening 5 Apr). Society-organised evenings outside the troupes' own seasons are not chronicled.
2. **Benefit evenings the review does name are performances that are already titled in the Repertoire:** 25 Jan Лакмэ (for the artists' refuge), 29 Jan morning Фаустъ (for the orchestra), 4 Apr Риголетто (Red Cross, Iverskaya community), 15 Apr Травіата (second režissers, prompters and librarians), 26 Apr Русалка (for the invalids).
3. **New fact for the Repertoire: 15 Apr 1906, Большой.** The review (folio 207) says the benefit gave "опера Верди «Травіата» и данъ былъ дивертиссементъ"; the Repertoire records only «Травіата» (annotation "Бенефисъ вторыхъ режиссеровъ, суфлеровъ и библіотекарей") with no divertissement.
4. **Ballet-company dancers credited inside operas (the crossover RG cares about), balletmaster A. А. Горскій:** «Панъ-воевода» (Rimsky-Korsakov), folio 192: Краковякъ in Act 1 (8 men named), Мазурка in Act 3 (Смирнова, Некрасова, Чернявская, Балашова ... and men), Казачекъ (5 men); «Аида», 1 Nov 1905 revival, folios 194-195: танецъ невольниковъ (7 men) and танецъ Алменей и невольниковъ (about 25 women and 7 men, all named). These names are candidates to link to the Moscow ballet troupe list in the people layer.
5. The Drama review has no ballet/dance mention apart from lines of the Tempest quoted for Ariel's "танцуйте" (folio 164) and plot summaries.

Nothing in the database was changed by this check. Folio digits were taken from the readers' reports and cross-checked by hand on folios 192, 194, 206, 207 (one digit I first misread on the rotated image, 194 read as 191, is 194).
