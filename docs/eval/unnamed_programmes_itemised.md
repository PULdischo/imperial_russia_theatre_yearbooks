# The 128 unnamed-programme events, matched against the reviews

The Repertoire tabulates date, theatre and receipts but prints the programme
item as a bare `Дивертиссементъ` / `Концертное отдѣленіе` / `Концертъ`. The
season reviews narrate the same evenings in prose and often itemize them, cast
and all — neither layer is recoverable from the other. Join key: season + city
+ date. Generated by `pipeline/match_unnamed_programmes.py`.

**Two cautions before using any of this.** The passages are quoted from the
review *transcription*, which is not a verbatim diplomatic text — check the
scan before quoting. And the join is date-exact but **not** event-exact: a
review may well be describing a different evening that happened to fall on the
same date. The `corroborated` column flags the entries where one of the
event's own named works also appears in the passage, which is the strong
signal; an uncorroborated match is a lead, not a fact.

## Result

| outcome | all | of which corroborated |
|---|---|---|
| itemised | 10 | **8** |
| partial | 19 | **15** |
| mentioned only | 8 | **5** |
| no review passage | 91 | — |

`itemised` = the passage shows three or more programme items (numbered
entries, quoted dance titles, French dance names). **8 events are both
itemised and corroborated** — those are the ones where the review demonstrably
supplies the programme the table withheld.

## Why 91 did not match

- **26** are at drama houses (Малый, Александринскій) or are charity and
  symphony concerts. Only ballet and some opera reviews have been scanned, so
  no review covers those evenings at all. A scanning-scope limit, not a
  matching failure.
- **65** are at ballet or opera houses whose review exists but never names
  that date. The reviews are selective rather than a chronicle: the 1903-04 SP
  ballet review names 27 distinct dates in the whole season, and only one of
  the six wanted there. Nothing to recover from the text we have.
