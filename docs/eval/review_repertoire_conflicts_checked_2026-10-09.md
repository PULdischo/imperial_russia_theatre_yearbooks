# The review-versus-Repertoire conflicts, checked on the Repertoire scans (2026-10-09)

For each conflict listed in `review_repertoire_matches_2026-10-09.md` and the `review_check_*` notes, the Repertoire page was rendered from `pdf/RepertoireTables/ForUpload_<season>_Repertoire.pdf` and the printed cell read (zoomed where marked). **Result: in all four cases our Repertoire rows are a faithful transcription of the printed table; the disagreement is between the yearbook's own two printed sources (the table and the review text).** Nothing in the database was changed.

## What this means for the data
- No Repertoire correction is needed. Where the review adds items (rows 2, 3), the cell content stays as printed; the review's extras belong in the research layer as sourced additions if RG decides to model them (see `research-layer-excerpts-and-mixed-events`).
- The reviews and the tables were compiled separately; their disagreements are themselves data about how the yearbook was made.
