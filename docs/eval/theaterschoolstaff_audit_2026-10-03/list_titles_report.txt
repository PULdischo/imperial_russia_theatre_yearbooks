OTHER
- In no season does the stored institution of row 1 equal the printed list title. It always equals the SPb school heading: exactly in 3 seasons (1891-92, 1892-93, 1894-95); in the other 17 it differs only by the missing final period (printed headings ALL end in "Училище."). So the raw institution value never carries the list title; the list title is not stored as any field I checked.
- Printed SPb heading uses "Императорское" in 1890-91 and 1895-96..1909-10 (16 seasons); omits it in 1891-92, 1892-93, 1893-94, 1894-95 (4 seasons). Spelling is "Императорское" (-ое) and "Петербургское" throughout in headings, but the list title uses "Императорскихъ Театральныхъ Училищахъ".
- Moscow heading is printed with the final period in all 20 seasons; the stored Moscow institution lacks it in 1891-92, 1893-94, 1894-95, 1900-01, 1904-05, 1908-09 (and 1909-10 p003/p004 has it).
- 1890-91 title omits "въ Императорскихъ Театральныхъ Училищахъ" and ends "служащихъ."; list is titled singular-school only in that volume (SPb heading immediately follows, then Moscow heading mid-p003).
- Image-folder stray duplicates: theaterschoolstaff_1899-90_p000.png is byte-identical (md5) to 1899-00_p000.png, and 1905-07_p000.png to 1905-06_p000.png (the 1899-90 and 1905-07 sets are typo-named copies).
- Show-through ghosts at the top of p000 (1890-91..1893-94, 1908-09, 1909-10) are the verso text bleeding through, not running heads. Running head "личный составъ." exists only on p001/p002 of 1908-09 and 1909-10 (p001 verified visually; p001 of 1893-94, 1900-01, 1907-08 checked: none).
- Possible raw-order/label anomaly (observed, not investigated): on p002 of 1898-99, 1901-02, 1902-03 and 1903-04 the stored entries start with Moscow rows (Управляющій Училищемъ ... Moscow label) while the printed top of that page is the SPb tail; e.g. 1902-03 p002 entry 1 "Учителя приготовительныхъ классовъ" and entries "35.", "13.", "14." carry the Moscow institution though they print above the Moscow heading. Worth a check against issue #126-129 heading_path work.
