# TheaterSchoolStaff: independent blind re-read of list titles and school headings (2026-10-05)

**Why.** The list title (stored as `institution`) and the school heading (first segment of `heading_path`) had been set from ONE reader per season in issue #134, and the later 22-page blind sample could only check a page against itself. RG asked to make this more robust.

**Method.** Every one of the 99 pages was re-read blind TWICE by independent report-only readers (pass A: pages interleaved over 5 readers; pass B: consecutive blocks over 5 different readers; `PROMPT.md`, `partitions.json`, raw reader output in `reader_results/`). Readers saw only the scan, never anything stored. Per page they transcribed the list title block, every school heading (letter-exact), the two entries each heading sits between, and which school the first and last entry of the page belong to. `compare.py` then compared reader A, reader B and the stored data three ways, plus a **continuity check** that carries the school forward page by page within a season (a page without a printed school heading belongs to the school in force at the end of the previous page).

**Results.**
- Titles: A, B and stored agree for all 20 seasons (the three title wordings: 1890-91 "Списокъ личнаго состава преподавателей и служащихъ."; 1891-92..1908-09 "... въ Императорскихъ Театральныхъ Училищахъ."; 1909-10 "Списокъ служащихъ при Императорскихъ Театральныхъ Училищахъ лицъ.").
- School heading texts: A and B agree on every heading and on whether a heading is printed, on all 99 pages (0 disagreements). Stored school names match the print, including the season-specific wording (Petersburg heading "С.-Петербургское Театральное Училище" without "Императорское" in 1891-92..1894-95; "Императорское Московское Театральное Училище" for Moscow throughout). Trailing periods vary in the print (e.g. none on the 1902-03 Moscow heading) and are typography, as in the settled conventions.
- Boundary rows: for all 58 pages-with-a-Moscow-heading checks, the entry before the heading is stored under Petersburg and the entry after it under Moscow (2 apparent misses were a ѳ/ф name-matching artefact, verified by hand).
- **One real error found: theaterschoolstaff_1898-99 p003 + p004 (48 rows) were stored under the Petersburg school but are the CONTINUATION of the Moscow list.** The Moscow heading is mid-page on p002 (11 Moscow rows, readers agree); p003 and p004 print no school heading and continue the Moscow list (p003 opens with Львова, Инспектриса, then the Moscow church staff, the doctor printed as "штатный врачъ при Московскихъ Императорскихъ театрахъ"; p004 is the Moscow teachers). All 48 people appear only under the Moscow school in every other season. Fixed (`apply_1898_99_school_fix.py`, only the first heading_path segment changes; backup `..._2026-10-05_school_1898_99`; rebuilt; Moscow rows for 1898-99 11 -> 59, Petersburg 123 -> 75, now in line with the neighbouring seasons). After the fix the continuity check reports 0 mismatches over all 99 pages.

**Lesson.** The earlier 22-page blind sample reported 0 heading/school errors, and that was true for what a page-local read can show -- but a continuation page prints no school heading, so a reader looking at that page alone cannot tell which school it belongs to (the sample's own readers noted this for several pages, and 1898-99 p004 was in the sample). Verifying school assignment needs the carry-forward check across pages, which is now `compare.py continuity`. The anomaly was visible in the stored data itself (123 Petersburg vs 11 Moscow rows in one season, against ~60 Moscow in every other); a per-season school-row count is a cheap check for any roster list with two sections.

**Not changed:** typographic differences (letter-spacing, line-end hyphenation in the title, running heads such as "ЛИЧНЫЙ СОСТАВЪ.", gathering marks) and verso show-through, which readers correctly ignored.
