Season 1904-05 is audited (p000-p005, 6 pages, 154 stored rows). I zoomed every page with PIL and read every crop; no files were edited. The full report with every shift row listed individually is at `/tmp/tss/results/1904-05.txt`, and the crops are in `/tmp/tss/crops/1904-05/`.

**Findings**
- **Missing rows:** none. Every printed person is among the stored rows.
- **Surname and name misreads (DIRECT):**
  - p000 e003: `Гогансонъ` should be `Іогансонъ`.
  - p001 e031: `Сенкусь` should be `Сенкусъ`.
  - p002 e003: `Тернизенъ`/`Эволь` should be `Тернизьенъ`/`Эводъ`.
  - p002 e010: `Щербачевичъ` should be `Щербацевичъ`.
  - p004 e025: `Никитфоровичъ` should be `Никифоровичъ`.
  - p004 e033: `Гутшеиѳъ` should be `Гутшейнъ`.
- **Ordinals dropped:** p001 e020 and e021 print `Легатъ 1-й` and `Легатъ 2-й`; the ordinal belongs in the surname.
- **Subject stored in tenure_note_text instead of subject_taught:** this is systematic wherever the print puts the subject after the date parenthetical. It affects p002 (25 rows), p004 (33 rows) and p005 (all 10 rows).
- **Printed notes dropped or mistyped:**
  - p002 e024 is missing "Оставилъ службу 1 сентября 1904 г."
  - p004 e029 is missing "Помощникъ преподавателя А. П. Ленскаго."
  - p005 e005 has `Иностранныя`; print is `Иностранная`.
- **Rank field:** p003 e001 has the office "Управляющій Московской Конторой..." in rank_or_title; in print it is the parenthetical after the name.
- **UNCERTAIN:** p005 e008 Стороженко start year. The last digit is a damaged glyph that looks like 4 (1904), stored as 1901.
- **Section-map:**
  - On p000, the sub-headings "Священникъ" and "Дьяконъ" are stored as person rows (e012, e014).
  - p000 e010 heading is stored `Инспектириса`; print has `Инспектриса`.
  - p001 e032-e034 (Воспитатели nos. 3-5, printed first on the page) are stored at the end of the array with `None` as heading_path.
  - p002 e001-e012 (nos. 25-36) are a continuation of "Преподаватели / а) Балетное отдѣленіе" but are stored under "Учителя приготовительныхъ классовъ".
  - All of p004 and p005 (45 rows, Moscow school) have institution = "Преподаватели:", so the school name from p003 is lost.
  - Smaller heading spellings: `Классные` (p001) and `Классеныя` (p003) versus printed `Классныя`, and `Причть` (p003) versus printed `Причтъ`.
- **Other:** later-dated entries (1905, 1906) appear in this volume and are stored as printed. p001 prints Голяховскій with patronymic Власьевичъ and p002 prints him as Васильевичъ. p002 e016 prints "1." for "г." (a genuine print typo).
