Season 1908-09 audit finished: p000-p004 (printed pp. 137-141), 113 stored rows, report only. I zoomed every page with PIL at 2.5x and read every crop: 48 crops in /tmp/tss/crops/1908-09/. The full report is also in /tmp/tss/results/1908-09.txt.

Main findings:
- **p000:** Row e009 stores the sub-heading "Дьяконъ" as a surname. The real person, "Смирновъ, Михаилъ Арсеньевичъ (съ 1 сентября 1903 г.)", is missing. Rows e001-e004 (honorary members) carry "съ 1 мая 1882 г.", but the print gives them no dates at all. The Священникъ/Дьяконъ sub-headings are lost.
- **p001:** Subject text is stored in rank_or_title for all of e006-e031.
  - e009: surname stored "Виршъ" but printed "Виршь"; the patronymic holds the date "съ 1 сентября 1908 г." although no patronymic is printed.
  - e020: the subject "Законъ Божій" is duplicated into the tenure field.
  - e023: first name stored "Янь", printed "Янъ".
  - e024: surname stored "Сенкусь", printed "Сенкусъ".
  - e001-e004 (nos. 3-6) are stored under "Классная дама при драматическихъ курсахъ", but they continue "Классныя дамы" from p000. That heading governs only Юргенсъ (e005).
- **p002:** Subject text is again stored in rank_or_title for e001 and e006-e016, with a trailing period. e014 reads "Иностранныя литература." but is printed "Иностранная литература.". Every row's heading_path holds the person's own number and name, and the sub-section "б) Драматическіе курсы" is lost for e009-e016.
- **p003:** No field errors. The only note is e001, where the parenthetical office note is merged with the rank in the rank field. Rows e017-e019 lost their parent heading "Классныя дамы".
- **p004:** e001-e020 hold the subject text inside tenure_note_text, with subject_taught empty. Row e005 prints "ВладимІръ" with a capital І mid-word, which I marked UNCERTAIN as a print oddity. The other stored fields match the print; e001, e002 and e020 differ from the print in the points below.
- **Institution field:**
  - p001 and p002: every row has "личный составъ" (the running head) as institution instead of the school.
  - p004: every row has "Преподаватели:" instead of the school.
  - The school is printed only on p000 (St Petersburg) and p003 (Moscow), so p001-p002 inherit it from p000 and p004 from p003.

Nothing else is missing or extra on p001-p004. Names, list numbers and dates not listed above match the print letter for letter.

Same-person print variants and several date-field oddities are listed under OTHER FINDINGS in the results file.
