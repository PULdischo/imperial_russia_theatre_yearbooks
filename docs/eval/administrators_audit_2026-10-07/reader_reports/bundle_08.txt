General conventions of this print (apply to every page):
 - The numeral "1" is printed in a small "I"-like form ("1 января", "1 апрѣля"); transcribed here as 1. Same for dates like "1903" (the "1" is that glyph).
 - Page folio printed at the lower right (125) or lower left (124, 128, 123) in the old numeral style.
 - "Оставилъ службу ..." lines are printed as an indented separate line under the person; the final letter of "Оставилъ" looks like a compressed "лъ" ligature; transcribed "Оставилъ".
 - "Назначенъ ..." lines are likewise indented separate lines belonging to the person above; they are included in the verbatim entry and in NOTE.
 - Headings are set in several type styles: (a) large thin sans/serif with swash initial (department level), (b) large sans-serif spanning both columns (Контора level), (c) small typewriter-like bold sans, centred (position level), (d) medium bold serif (Канцелярскіе чиновники Конторы:). Level numbers below: H1 = Контора, H2 = department (отдѣленіе / часть), H3 = position / class / theatre sub-heading.
