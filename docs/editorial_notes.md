# Editors' notes in the Ежегодникъ — finding list

Compiled 2026-10-19 by reading every scanned front- and end-matter page
(258 pages in the `*_Frontpages*` / `*_Endpages*` PDFs under
`Imperial Yearbooks Project/Yearbook Scans - Originals/`), plus a search of
the transcribed season-review text and the other filed PDFs for the
editors' voice («редакція», «Отъ редакціи», «Прим. ред.»). Headings are
quoted verbatim; summaries are in English. "PDF p." = page within the
named PDF.

Full Russian text with English translation, side by side, for every note
below (A–D, plus the 1909 Вып. III re-print of the XVIII announcement):
`docs/editorial_notes_bilingual.md`. It is built from
`docs/editorial_notes/text.json` by `pipeline/build_editorial_notes_bilingual.py`.

Scope limit: this covers **only what has been scanned**. Unscanned front/end
matter (and most «Объявленія» sections) may hold more notes — see the last
section.

## D. Minor unsigned publisher's notes (illustration credits)

Listed for completeness; they explain the sources of uncredited illustrations and who made the printing blocks.

- 1890-91 `Endpages_Illustrations.pdf` p. 5 (printed 328): blocks by Яблонскій, Демчинскій; Бокъ troupe portraits made abroad.
- 1893-94 `Endpages.pdf` p. 4 (printed 490), and the same notice in 1894-95 (p. 448), 1895-96 (p. 524), 1896-97 (p. 481), 1897-98 (p. 453): uncredited illustrations come from the Imperial theatres' photo studios, with К. А. Фишеръ for Moscow portraits; blocks by Ангереръ и Гёшль (Vienna), plus Яблонскій in some years.
- 1893-94 `Endpages_SupplementContents.pdf` p. 1 (printed 491): notes that three separate supplement books came out with the 4th issue.
