# Audit, 2026-10-10

Where the jubilee/obituary catalogue stands, what it can be relied on for, and what it cannot.

## What exists

`jubilees_obituaries_all_entries.csv`: 452 rows, 424 of them person entries.

| Source | Person entries | Core | Mentions | None |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scanned sections and stand-alone articles | 353 | 87 | 19 | 247 |
| Notices in the Season Reviews | 71 | 51 | 5 | 15 |
| Total | 424 | 138 | 24 | 262 |

By kind (all tiers): obituary 266, jubilee 102, memorial feature 21, farewell 19, death notice 10,
biographical feature 6. `jubilees_obituaries_ballet_only.csv` holds the 162 core and mentions people plus three
1890-92 «Смѣсь» articles.

## Checks run in this audit

- Every page of every scanned jubilee/obituary PDF is accounted for by an entry; the only uncovered
  page is the blank vignette verso at the end of `1893-94_Jubilees.pdf`.
- No duplicate rows; no person row without a name; every core/mentions row has an evidence quote;
  no word mixes Cyrillic with Latin or Greek letters.
- One name still carries an uncertain reading: Литавкинъ, Спиридонъ[?] Александровичъ (1890-91,
  damaged type; the portrait caption reads «С. А. Литавкинъ»).
- Defect found and fixed: issue-era rows carried a render name, not the real file path
  (now `1909 Vol VI-VII/1908-09_Jubilees.pdf` etc.).

   a «†» in the rosters; 13 of them have no obituary and no review mention, so the catalogue lacks
   them: Брыкинъ, Морозова (Е. М.), Скорсюкъ, Шебергъ, Ячменева (1900-01); Симонова (1902-03);
   Александровъ, Рославлева, Тройниковъ (1904-05); Иванова 2-я, Константиновъ, Рябовъ А. П. (1906-07);
   and М. И. Петипа («† 1 іюля 1910 г.», 1909-10 roster). The orchestra, school, production and
   administration rosters carry another 169 death marks, not cross-checked at all.
2. **[CLOSED 2026-10-10 — two wider searches run (40 + 87 further blocks read); nine notices added from `first_read/R2_reviews_wider_search.csv`. A notice using none of the searched words could still be missed.]** The review sweep was a keyword search and missed things. A wider pattern finds at least:
   Яковлевъ 2-й († 9 августа 1892 г., 1891-92 SP ballet review); Кламротъ's send-off on leaving
   service (1900-01 Moscow opera); Салина 15 years; Римскій-Корсаковъ 30 years; the last appearances
   of the guest ballerinas Дель-Эра (1892-93) and Замбелли (1901-02); the Bolshoi Theatre's 75th
   anniversary (1899-00). None is in the catalogue yet. A notice worded without any of the
   searched words would still be missed.
3. **Reviews not in the database cannot have been searched**: 1898-99, 1909-10, 1911-12, most
   opera reviews, all drama reviews.
4. **Unscanned pages** — see `unscanned_worth_getting.md`. Supplements unchecked (deferred).
5. **Farewells and jubilees exist only where the Yearbook wrote them up.** The catalogue is a
   record of what is printed, not of every jubilee or death in the troupes.

## Loose ends in other data, noticed here

- Roster `balletartists_1894-95` reads «Доровеевъ»; obituary and review print Дороѳеевъ.
- Roster 1890-91 reads Ахмакова «Калерія»; every later season «Клавдія».
- Four print conflicts between review and obituary (README, "Review death lists"); the review side
  has not been re-checked on the review scans.
- Per-ballet drawing counts in the Всеволожской article were not zoomed.
- `raw.review_block` for `review_1900-01_MSK_opera_p007` block 3 reads «¼ года»; the page prints «44 года» (Кламротъ's years as concertmaster). The catalogue row is corrected; the review transcription is not.

## Full read of the reviews — done 2026-10-11

Every transcribed review block was read (3,790 blocks; ballet, mixed and opera reviews), by 14
readers judging by meaning. 188 flags, 124 already in the catalogue, 64 read in context, 14 rows
added and one tier changed (see README, "Notices from the Season Reviews"). The readers found no
jubilee, farewell benefit or death of a member of the ballet troupe that the keyword searches had
missed, apart from Ауэръ's last participation. The review side is now complete for the reviews that
are in the database; it still rests on that transcription, and reviews not in the database
(1898-99, 1909-10, 1911-12, most opera, all drama) remain out of reach.

Transcription oddities the readers noticed, not checked: «2-го января 1903 года» inside the 1901-02
SP ballet review (p023 #2); «14 февраля 1910 г.» for Палечекъ inside the 1910-11 opera review;
«М. Петина» for Петипа (1902-03 SP ballet p019 #3); «успѣшаго» for Чайковскій (1893-94 SP opera p039 #4).
