# Jubilees and obituaries not yet scanned

What the Yearbooks contain in the way of jubilees, obituaries and memorial pieces that is **not**
in `Yearbook Scans - Originals`, ranked by how likely it is to matter for ballet.

Sources: the contents pages and annual indexes (`first_read/T1`, `T2`), the per-season sheets of
*Imperial Yearbook Inventory.xlsx* (which list each volume's full contents), partial entries seen at
the edges of the scanned extracts, and the Summary sheet for where each volume is held. "Held"
is as the Inventory stood on 2026-10-10. Ballet relevance here is judged from titles and names
only; nothing in this list has been read.

## 2. The annual closing issues, 1910–1914 — check when they are scanned

In the two issue-era years scanned so far, the last issue of the year carries a «Некрологи» section,
a «Юбилеи» section and the annual index (1909 VI–VII; 1911 VII). If the pattern holds, the same
sections are in:

| Issue | Held | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1910 VII and VIII | ILL (UMich), on loan to 2026-12-11 | Not yet scanned. Would cover deaths and jubilees of 1909–10. |
| 1912 VII | Princeton | Marked "Up next". |
| 1913 VII | ILL (UMich), on loan to 2026-12-11 | Marked "Up next". |
| 1914 VII | ILL (UMich), on loan to 2026-12-06 | |

For each: shoot the contents page and the annual index first, then the two sections whole — the
ballet entries are scattered through them alphabetically and cannot be picked out from the
contents alone (1911 VII: 7 ballet entries among 49).

Also unseen, with no contents page yet: 1910 I–V, 1911 I–II, 1912 I–VI, 1913 V–VI, 1914 I–VI.
Single memorial articles can sit in any issue (e.g. 1909 III, Кони; 1913 I, Самойловъ).

## 4. Seasons with no jubilee or obituary section at all

The Inventory sheets list the full contents of these main volumes and show none:
1896-97 (apart from the Петипа article, scanned), 1898-99, 1902-03, 1903-04, 1905-06
("no special features, obituaries, jubilees"). 1901-02 has only the Л. Ивановъ obituary in
Supplement 3 (scanned). So the ballet deaths the reviews report for 1896-97 and 1897-98
(Разуевъ, Троицкая, Ахмакова, Литавкинъ, Дмитріевъ) have no obituary to find — the review line and
the roster dagger are all the Yearbook prints. The supplements (приложенія) for these years have not been checked for obituaries and may hold
some. That is deferred: RG, 2026-10-10 — supplements come after the repertoire-data layer.
Planned first pass when it happens: read each supplement's contents page on HathiTrust, and request
a physical copy only where something ballet-related shows up.
