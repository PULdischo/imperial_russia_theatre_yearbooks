## Row 09 — Новый opera, 38 events v. printed 37
All three free school matinées are in the list on their dates: «Трубадуръ» ноября 14, «Рафаэль» / «Алеко» /
«Сельская честь» декабря 6, «Вертеръ» февраля 24. The list therefore counts all three as performed; it does not
show which one (if any) the stats page left out. **The list cannot settle row 09**; the 37 remains the stats page's own.

## Row 14 — Александринскій, 174 events v. printed 175 (+2,709.50 р.)
Every Александринскій date in the Repertoire is in the list except 1 Jan 1905 «Крылья связаны» (a charity event with no
